Vitrimers combine the strength of thermosets with the ability to be reshaped and reused like thermoplastics. This handbook provides a clear overview of vitrimer chemistry, how they are made, how they are tested, and where they are used, linking research with practical applications. Written by experts from around the world, it covers dynamic covalent bonding, modern testing methods, and examples from industries such as composites, electronics, and additive manufacturing.
