## ABSTRACT Polymer sustainability is commonly evaluated through feedstock selection, renewable materials, and chemical design; however, the role of polymer processing is often overlooked when determining environmental impact. Existing sustainability frameworks, including the principles of Green Chemistry and Green Engineering, provide important guidance for chemical and industrial development but do not fully address the unique challenges associated with polymer processing, such as high thermal and mechanical energy input, process complexity, scalability, and industrial implementation.
