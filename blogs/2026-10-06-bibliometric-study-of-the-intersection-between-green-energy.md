The rapid convergence of artificial intelligence (AI) and green energy (GE) is transforming sustainable business practices, energy management, and economic development. Although research in this interdisciplinary field has expanded considerably, its intellectual structure, thematic evolution, and business implications remain insufficiently understood. This study presents a comprehensive bibliometric analysis of AI and GE research from a business and economics perspective.
