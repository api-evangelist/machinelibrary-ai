## Abstract Within the framework of urban sustainability at the architectural scale, climate-responsive and sustainable interior design has become increasingly pivotal. While existing research addresses indoor environmental quality (IEQ) through parameters such as thermal, visual, and acoustic comfort, these studies often remain fragmented and focused on isolated performance metrics. Furthermore, there is a significantly limited amount of research that integrates human-centric perspectives with data-driven methodologies within the domain of climate-responsive interior design.
