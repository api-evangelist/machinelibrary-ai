Min-Plus Convolution is a central problem in fine-grained complexity, and the associated Min-Plus Convolution Hypothesis forms the basis for a wide range of conditional lower bounds for fundamental problems. It is closely connected to the APSP and 3SUM Hypotheses, and in fact implies both, making it a unifying hypothesis for two of the main pillars of the area. In this work we establish several strong results related to Min-Plus Convolution.
