Although urban heat is commonly addressed through local adaptation and urban heat reduction measures such as urban greening, cool and super cool roofs, and building retrofits or passive design, these interventions are increasingly implemented against a backdrop of accelerating planetary warming, fiscal constraints, and persistent socio-economic inequalities.
