This convergent mixed-methods study investigates the strategic integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) into a university-level STEAM curriculum to empower students to design climate solutions. Combining quantitative survey data from 69 undergraduate students selected via a complete cohort census sampling approach (response rate: 84.1%) and multi-tiered purposive qualitative interviews with __N__ = 25 participants (__N__ = 11 experts, __N__ = 9 faculty members, and __N__ = 5 student innovators), the findings indicate high technological familiarity and widespread adoption (100% AI tool use).
