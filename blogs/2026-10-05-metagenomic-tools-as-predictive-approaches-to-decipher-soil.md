The increasing demand for resilient and sustainable food production necessitates a comprehensive understanding of rhizosphere microbial communities and their functional roles in enhancing plant health, nutrient acquisition, stress resilience, and crop productivity, thereby supporting food security and sustainable agriculture. However, soil microbiomes are composed of complex and unculturable communities of beneficial microorganisms that drive critical ecosystem functions, which traditional culture methods cannot capture.
