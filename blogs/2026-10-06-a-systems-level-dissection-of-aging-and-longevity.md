## Abstract Aging is a complex biological process influenced by numerous molecular and cellular factors. A systems-level understanding of these mechanisms is essential for identifying key regulatory processes and prioritizing effective interventions. Here, we present a comprehensive dissection of aging and longevity mechanisms powered by an evidence-routed AI-based knowledge graph, HALDxAI.
